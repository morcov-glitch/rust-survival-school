A first-person training island that walks you through the real loop of Rust — gather, craft, bag up, hunt, build, secure, survive — with hints on everything you look at. Wild animals and hostile players roam the island and they will come for you. The Field Guide is the deep reference: resources, cloth, sleeping bags, base defence, raid costs and how to survive mid-wipe without getting stuck on the coast. Numbers follow the real game where it matters; treat exact values as approximate. Best with a mouse and keyboard.
